German Business Kit: AI skills for running a small business in Germany (Claude & Codex walkthrough)

## Description

Running a small business in Germany means paperwork, often in a second language. German Business Kit is a set of seven open-source skills for Claude and Codex that helps sole traders (Freiberufler and Gewerbe) with registration worksheets, invoices, receipts and bank matching, tax preparation, Finanzamt replies, choosing services, and later changes or closing. You ask in plain English, German or Russian, and it keeps documents, confirmed facts and next steps in one private business folder.

The rule behind it: it prepares, you decide and send. You sign in to ELSTER, your bank or your accounting software yourself, and you make the final click.

The video includes three real questions, asked in fresh sessions with the released kit (0.13.0-preview.2): a translator in Berlin (Freiberufler or Gewerbe?), a web designer in Hamburg (Kleinunternehmerregelung) and a photographer in Munich (lowering tax prepayments). You see the actual answers, shortened, with the official sources they opened. The people are fictional; the answers are real.
