# Surface brief · launch carousel, LinkedIn video, YouTube walkthrough

Mode: Persuade. Audience: freelancers and sole traders in Germany, many of them expats reading in a second language (see PRODUCT.md). Editions: English and German carousels with real answers asked in each language; videos in English.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every answer comes with its receipt. The surface refuses the AI-launch default (cream ground, serif display, terracotta accent; or dark neon) and shows the kit's mechanism as the most ordinary German business artifact: a Beleg that itemizes what was checked and ends with who sends.

OWN-WORLD: Carbon-paper blue ground. Receipts on original white and duplicate-book copy tints (canary yellow, pale blue, pink), torn serrated edges, dashed and double rules, dot leaders, right-aligned values, a working QR code. Black thermal ink plus red second ink only for limits, deadlines and "YOU". One monospace family; emphasis only through double-height and double-width print, never through tiny secondary type. A red GEPRÜFT/ENTWURF stamp is the only overlay.

STORY: The visitor sees a familiar receipt, recognizes German paperwork, reads three real questions itemized like purchases with their official sources ticked, and learns the one rule printed as the total: sent by you. They try it with a made-up example.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Cover slide: a tall white receipt slightly over a yellow and pink copy, centred on carbon blue; double-height "Every answer comes with its receipt."; four ticked lines; double rule; "SENT BY ...... YOU" in double width with YOU in red; barcode; meta strip above reads the brand and BELEG 01/12.

FORM: Kassenbon (thermal receipt and duplicate-book copies), position 6 of the grounded list, seed key 291c5f8f. Raises: one type size per slide body, rank by rules and position (from the cutting-bench challenger); the current or decisive line prints darkest, earlier lines fade like thermal paper (from the ink-basin challenger); each city prints on its own copy tint (from the sleeping-city challenger); BELEG numbering runs through all slides as one roll (from the cyclorama challenger). In video, receipts print line by line.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
