# Choose services that fit your business

[English](service-selection.md) | [Русский](service-selection.ru.md) | [Documentation](index.md)

Ask the assistant to research and recommend banking, insurance or other business services based on how you actually work. It reuses your known needs and existing services, asks for the few missing facts that affect the choice, and compares current evidence. You do not need to create a workspace or buy another service before getting useful research.

| Request | What the comparison covers |
| --- | --- |
| Business bank account or payment service | Eligibility, account name, cash/cards, currencies and transfer volumes, normal fees and FX, funds protection, support and accounting integration |
| Business insurance | Existing cover, actual activity and client countries, legal or contractual requirements, insured risks, exclusions, limits, deductibles, dates and total premium |
| Accounting, invoicing or payment software | The work you need to perform, bank feeds/API/export, e-invoices, portability, data handling and total plan cost |
| Hosting, email, communications, storage or backup | Necessary features, ownership/access, support, renewal and exit cost, export, recovery and relevant data-processing terms |

Examples:

- “Compare business accounts for my existing activity. I receive EUR and USD, need no cash deposits, and want a bank feed into my accounting software. Show first-year and normal annual costs.”
- “I develop software for German and US clients. Compare my current professional-liability cover with these offers and identify exclusions or conditions that matter before choosing.”
- “Review the services I already pay for. What should I keep, change or avoid buying, given my budget and actual needs?”

The result is a short requirements list, an evidence-based comparison, a cost calculation and a recommendation with reasons. Material unknowns remain visible. A cheap option that fails an essential requirement does not win by price alone; keeping the existing service or buying nothing can be the right choice. First-year promotions are separated from normal renewal costs, and missing fees are not treated as zero.

For insurance, the assistant distinguishes statutory duties, client-contract requirements and optional protection. It compares actual policy wording and endorsements rather than treating a marketing label as confirmed cover. Public “from” prices, individual quotes and accepted policies remain separate. Detailed medical information and identity documents are not needed for an initial shortlist. Commercial insurance advice/distribution and purchase-linked comparisons have their own regulatory boundaries; the skill does not establish a licensed role. See the [official-source notes](../skills/de-business-kit/references/service-research-sources.md).

With a private workspace, the assistant saves a readable comparison and relevant open questions or decision records. The user can approve choices in conversation; no JSON editing is required. Research does not apply for accounts, send personal data for quotes, purchase subscriptions, cancel policies or move existing records. Those actions require a concrete reviewed scope and applicable authorization, followed by actual provider confirmation.

Provider terms and personal needs change. Ask for a refresh before relying on an older comparison or renewing/switching; a dated task is not an automatic monitoring service. Market coverage and any relevant commercial relationship should be disclosed. Current source access, full market coverage and individual acceptance must never be invented.
