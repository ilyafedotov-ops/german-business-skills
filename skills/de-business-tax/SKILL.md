# Business Tax

Read the [shared context and execution contract](../de-business-kit/references/shared-context.md)
on direct entry. This skill ships with the complete German Business Kit bundle;
shared resources remain in the sibling `de-business-kit` directory. Read only
references relevant to the task. Use the user's language and saved context.

Use [tax preparation](../de-business-kit/references/tax-consultant.md) and
[German rules](../de-business-kit/references/german-rules.md) for the actual year
and facts. Answer an ordinary question without requiring setup or month-end work.
For filling or reviewing a real portal draft, use
[assisted service operations](../de-business-kit/references/assisted-services.md)
with [forms](../de-business-kit/references/forms.md). Use available authorized
browser/computer tools for preparation and readback; honor a user-held final
confirmation instead of either refusing all assistance or submitting silently.

- Separate known facts, user assumptions, current evidence and unanswered issues.
  Use [source-led calculations and independent checks](../de-business-kit/references/tax-estimates.md);
  Establish sufficient inputs before calculation. Use a verified official/provider
  tool or a bounded calculation derived from inspected applicable rules; never
  invent formulas, rates, thresholds, official field mappings or results.
- For cross-border facts read [international](../de-business-kit/references/international.md).
  Do not infer tax treatment merely from a bank location, currency or filename.
  For EU goods use its before-order or received-invoice checks; distinguish
  foreign invoice VAT, acquisition tax and Kleinunternehmer ID-use consequences.
- For reducing income-tax prepayments use the [saved request workflow](../de-business-kit/references/tax-consultant.md#prepare-or-revise-an-income-tax-prepayment-request):
  inspect the notice and forecast, compute the proposed adjustment, save the German
  draft and separate private review, then preserve versions when facts change.
- For monthly or annual returns use [annual workflow](../de-business-kit/references/annual-workflow.md),
  [forms](../de-business-kit/references/forms.md) and the existing
  [filing evidence lifecycle](../de-business-kit/references/accounting-workflow.md).
  Confirm the chosen calculation tool supports the form/year. Preserve actual
  inputs/results and distinguish manually entered, observed and not-run outputs.
- To continue a saved filing, inspect its actual state and run the [filing continuation checks](../de-business-kit/references/accounting-workflow.md#check-before-advancing-a-filing).
  Carry receipt/preparation evidence into the exact version; resolve changed
  inputs with linked replacements and uncertain attempts with outcome evidence.
- For authority correspondence prepare a chronology, source-backed deadline
  assessment, evidence list and recipient-language draft under
  [correspondence](../de-business-kit/references/correspondence.md) and
  [document authoring](../de-business-kit/references/document-authoring.md).
  A receipt is not an authority decision. If sending timed out, investigate the
  existing attempt before any repeat; preserve uncertainty.

Complete a useful explanation or reviewable filing/reply package. Reuse existing
specific authorization at the external boundary; record sending and outcome only
from observed evidence. No required accountant handoff; use focused professional
help when a particular unresolved issue needs it. Accounting corrections go to
`de-business-accounting`, with the exact finding and affected period.
