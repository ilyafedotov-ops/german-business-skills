# German rules: working reference

Checked: **2026-10-25**. Scope: a natural person running service activities in Germany as an Einzelunternehmer, including freiberufliche and gewerbliche activities. This is a dated source map, not a determination of an individual's tax position. Recheck the applicable official text, effective date and reporting period before a material tax choice, deadline calculation or submission. Record the check and its source; do not replace it with the date this file was written.

## Route work without losing useful progress

Continue collecting facts, organising documents, checking arithmetic, preparing field worksheets and identifying specific questions when a tax issue is unresolved. Hold only the affected tax conclusion or external action. Distinguish factual extraction, a proposed treatment, a user decision, an adviser decision and an authority determination. Never silently turn a suggestion into an approved posting.

## Recover an unavailable primary source

Use this sequence for material legal rules, calculations and official procedures:

1. Open the specific official provision or guidance and inspect its relevant text,
   including conditions, exceptions and application provisions. Search results are
   discovery aids, not proof that the source was read.
2. If that page fails or returns incomplete text, try the publisher's full-act HTML,
   official consolidated PDF or linked official alternative. Locate the exact
   section and inspect its context; use PDF extraction or page inspection as needed.
   For an older period retrieve the applicable version, not merely today's text.
3. An equivalent primary authority publication can support the same claim only
   when its scope, wording and applicable period actually cover it. A mirror, cached
   snippet or secondary summary is not automatically equivalent. Do not evade an
   access control or assume a newer PDF applies retroactively.
4. Record the failed route, successful URL/title/section, actual retrieval date,
   visible version, applicable period and any remaining gap in the existing
   consultation/source evidence. Do not report a successful source check for an
   inaccessible page. Where available, keep the inspected extract/export with its
   source identity; a URL alone is not a preserved copy.
5. If the alternatives still leave a material condition unverified, name that
   condition and keep the affected conclusion provisional or unresolved. Continue
   independent factual preparation. Do not fill the gap with remembered law or
   snippets, or add peripheral legal claims whose sources were not checked.

“Full relevant text” means enough of the actual primary document to establish the
rule and its qualifications; it does not require reading an entire statute for
every question. Retrieval success and applicability are separate checks.

For companies, partnerships, complex payroll, cash-register operations, regulated activities, insolvency, foreign establishments or complex cross-border transactions, prepare a factual handoff and route the specialised question outside this initial release. Do not claim those cases are covered merely because the files can be created.

## Keep four independent classifications

| Dimension | Required distinction | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Legal form | Natural person / other entity | The initial release supports natural persons; a brand name does not create a separate legal entity. |
| Activity | Freiberuflich / gewerblich / mixed / unresolved | Assess the actual services and qualifications; do not let the user choose a classification solely to obtain a tax result. |
| VAT | Kleinunternehmer / Regelbesteuerung / unresolved | This is separate from activity classification and bookkeeping method. |
| Profit determination | EÜR / Bilanzierung / unresolved | Determine statutory obligations and any voluntary method already used. |

Under §2 UStG the VAT enterprise encompasses the entrepreneur's entire commercial and professional activity. Gather other activities and their VAT history before evaluating §19 UStG. A new Gewerbe, project, trading name or tax-registration questionnaire does not automatically reset the natural person's existing VAT position. Record the activity-level data separately from the person-level VAT assessment. [UStG §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__2.html); [EStG §18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__18.html)

## Registration and deadlines

- A person beginning a standing Gewerbe must notify the competent authority at commencement under §14 GewO. Check the municipality's actual procedure, required permits and fees.
- ELSTER states that the Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung must be transmitted electronically, without waiting for a request, within one month after commencing the activity. Gewerbeanmeldung does not replace this questionnaire.
- Identify the legally relevant trigger date from the facts. Keep preparation, actual commencement, public launch, registration and submission dates distinct. Do not implement “one month” as an unconditional 30-day offset. Verify applicable deadline-computation rules and any extensions before treating a date as final.

Sources: [GewO §14](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__14.html); [ELSTER formation guidance](https://www.elster.de/elsterweb/infoseite/unternehmensgruendung); [ELSTER questionnaire help](https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_fseeun_202401).

## VAT snapshot: verify before applying

The retrieved §19 UStG uses **€25,000** qualifying prior-year turnover and **€100,000** current-year turnover limits. Qualifying Gesamtumsatz has its own statutory definition; it is not every bank inflow. For a genuinely new entrepreneurial activity, official state guidance describes a **€25,000 founding-year limit**, with the crossing transaction already subject to normal taxation. This founding-year rule must not be applied merely because an existing entrepreneur adds another activity.

A voluntary waiver of §19 binds the entrepreneur for at least five calendar years. Ask for existing VAT treatment, prior waivers, dates, relevant authority correspondence and turnover from all activities before drafting a selection. Treat uncertain history or approaching a threshold as a specific review question. Do not round a near-threshold result or use an unverified estimate as a legal conclusion.

Sources: [UStG §19](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__19.html); [Finanzverwaltung Hessen: Kleinunternehmer](https://finanzamt.hessen.de/steuern/umsatzsteuer-kleinunternehmer); [BMF 18 March 2025](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/Downloads/BMF_Schreiben/Steuerarten/Umsatzsteuer/Umsatzsteuer-Anwendungserlass/2025-03-18-sonderregelung-kleinunternehmer.pdf?__blob=publicationFile&v=4).

Kleinunternehmer status does not by itself remove obligations when purchasing foreign services. §13b can make the recipient liable. For foreign SaaS, advertising or AI invoices, record supplier establishment, recipient status, service, relevant dates, amounts and invoice tax treatment; flag the VAT question instead of assuming “no VAT”. Foreign sales require a separate review of service type, customer status and place-of-supply rules. Do not assume OSS or EU small-business arrangements apply automatically. [UStG §13b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__13b.html)

## EÜR and bookkeeping

EÜR under §4(3) EStG is available subject to the absence of a statutory bookkeeping obligation and the taxpayer not voluntarily keeping books and preparing accounts. It still requires appropriate records, including applicable asset and depreciation records. For commercial businesses, §141 AO contains thresholds exceeding **€800,000** turnover or **€80,000** profit; its notification and commencement rules matter. Do not apply these thresholds indiscriminately to Freiberufler or switch methods automatically on the crossing date. HGB §241a has a separate exemption for qualifying Einzelkaufleute.

Sources: [EStG §4](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__4.html); [AO §141](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__141.html); [HGB §241a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/hgb/__241a.html).

Use the workspace as preparation and evidence support. The selected accounting system and documented business process remain material to compliance. A generated CSV is not a filed return, approved ledger or proof of correct tax treatment.

## E-invoices

The BMF FAQ retrieved with a March 2026 update describes:

- Reception of structured E-Rechnungen from **1 January 2025**, including for Kleinunternehmer. An email inbox can suffice for reception.
- General issuance transition through **2026**, extended through **2027** where the issuer's prior-year turnover does not exceed **€800,000**. Check remaining exceptions before applying the post-transition rule.
- An issuance exception for Kleinunternehmer and other specified transactions; B2C and B2G require their own scope checks.
- Ordinary PDF is not the structured E-Rechnung format. Preserve the original structured content; OCR, screenshots and normalised JSON are derivatives.

Separate technical format validation, arithmetic checks and substantive tax review. For hybrid documents inspect the XML and relevant attachments, not just the displayed PDF. Use maintained validators where available; never label a parser or an LLM review as certified compliance. [BMF E-Rechnung FAQ](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/FAQ/e-rechnung.html)

## Preservation, GoBD and retention

GoBD paragraphs 107–111 require traceable changes. A normal filesystem generally needs additional measures to ensure immutability; a folder structure, hashes or Git alone do not demonstrate overall GoBD conformity. Maintain originals, provenance, controlled corrections, access rules, backups and a documented procedure. Do not claim a DMS brand alone guarantees compliance. [Official GoBD text, AO handbook 2025](https://amtliche-handbuecher.bundesfinanzministerium.de/ao/2025/Anhaenge/BMF-Schreiben-und-gleichlautende-Laendererlasse/Anhang-33/anhang-33.html)

Avoid the opposite overstatement: BMF FAQ question 13 says storing E-Rechnungen outside a GoBD-compliant system does not, by that fact alone, normally violate §14b UStG or content integrity for VAT purposes. That limited point is not a waiver of other recordkeeping requirements. [BMF FAQ](https://www.bundesfinanzministerium.de/Content/DE/FAQ/e-rechnung.html)

| Record class under the retrieved §147 AO | Base retention |
|---|---:|
| Books, records, inventories, accounts and the other documents in §147(1) nos. 1 and 4a | 10 years |

The period generally starts at the relevant calendar-year end. Extensions, open assessment periods and other applicable requirements can prevent destruction. Invoices have a separate eight-year rule in §14b UStG. Record a retention class and unresolved hold; do not implement automatic deletion from this table. [AO §147](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ao_1977/__147.html); [UStG §14b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14b.html)

## Free software and individual tax services

The retrieved StBerG text reflects amendments dated 29 June 2026. Recheck its effective provisions for the actual operating model rather than copying older paragraph numbers. §2 covers assistance in others' tax affairs requiring an individual legal examination and addresses paid and unpaid activity. Its mechanical-work exclusion does not include allocating receipts to accounts or giving posting instructions. Current §6 contains qualified bookkeeping and conditional unpaid-assistance exceptions, including supervision conditions for assistance to non-relatives.

Publishing open-source software is not automatically the same as personally providing a regulated tax service; nor does a free price or disclaimer settle the classification. Keep product claims accurate and distinguish self-help tooling from services offered by maintainers. If the project later offers individual tax determinations, representation or managed bookkeeping, review that service model specifically. These questions do not block preparing factual records and draft documents.

Sources: [StBerG §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stberg/__2.html); [§5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stberg/__5.html); [§6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stberg/__6.html); [current law/version information](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/stberg/BJNR013010961.html).
