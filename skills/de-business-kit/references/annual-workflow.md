# Annual reporting, corrections and authority follow-up

Use for a chosen accounting system and actual tax year. This is agent-led
preparation using existing records and external tools, not a statutory closing
or return-calculation engine. Read [accounting workflow](accounting-workflow.md),
[forms](forms.md), [source-led estimates](tax-estimates.md) and relevant current
[official sources](lifecycle-sources.md). Start a durable readiness report with
[EN annual preparation](../assets/en/annual-readiness.md) or
[RU annual preparation](../assets/ru/annual-readiness.md). Adapt it to actual
coverage; do not fill every row merely to make a report look complete.

## Prepare the period

Identify the entrepreneur's activities, year/period, method and existing decisions.
Determine applicable forms and deadlines from case-specific current sources;
do not treat the local form catalog as proof of a current complete form set.
Reuse existing books and recorded submissions instead of reconstructing them from
bank receipts. For an external system, inspect supported exports and actual state.

Gather a bounded evidence inventory: period records, unresolved questions,
reconciliations, corrections, prior submissions and authority correspondence.
Identify assets, noncash items, mixed/private use, carryovers or other adjustments
that the limited cash journal cannot establish. Ask for the relevant authoritative
record or selected tool workflow; never fill a missing adjustment with zero.

Provide a readable readiness report: supported inputs, missing evidence, pending
decisions, required external steps and the next action. Local cash totals alone
cannot be labelled the complete EÜR, taxable income or VAT payable.

## Prepare and submit through the chosen route

Verify the actual tool edition, form and year, permissions and input mapping.
Record which inputs were entered, actual output, tool/settings and whether the
result was observed, user-supplied or not run. Investigate discrepancies without
averaging results or inventing a fallback tax calculator.

Prepare the exact return draft and attachments, separate review and authorization,
and use the existing filing preflight/evidence lifecycle when supported. A user
can submit manually with assistance; do not require a proprietary connector.
Keep ambiguous attempts visible across periods and inspect submission history
before retrying. A saved PDF or local status does not prove transmission.

## Response or correction

Identify the actual sender, notice/version, affected activity/form/year, receipt
facts, stated deadline and requested action. Verify deadline applicability and
any calculation through current sources; do not infer service from a filename.
Compare the notice against the submitted version and supporting evidence.

Prepare a factual difference list, missing evidence, user-language explanation
and a recipient-language reply/correction draft. Distinguish an explanation,
corrected return, objection and other procedural routes; research the applicable
route before proposing its legal effect. Preserve prior filings and deadlines.
Use focused professional help for an unresolved issue while continuing unaffected
preparation. Record sending and authority response only when evidenced.

## Continue when inputs change

Reopen the latest report and relevant filing record, then compare each referenced
input version with current evidence. List which figures/fields need a fresh tool
run; do not carry forward a previous estimate as verified. If a filing attempt is
unconfirmed, retain it even when preparing a different period or revised draft.
A supplied receipt should be checked against the actual taxpayer reference,
form/year and attempt; a different period's receipt cannot resolve this attempt.
Use the existing writers/preflight for supported saved filings, and keep a readable
note when the chosen external system owns the result. Never edit state JSON to
force a review or manufacture a receipt. Authority responses remain separate from
transmission evidence; use [correspondence](correspondence.md) for the reply.
