# Lifecycle source map

Checked **2026-10-26** using live official-source searches and page reads. This
shared agent reference supports [lifecycle routing](lifecycle.md); it is not a
complete legal guide, a filing integration or evidence that any user action was
completed. Sources below describe current pages or consolidated law as checked;
for an earlier or later event, check the applicable version and transitional
provisions. No case-specific deadline, tax calculation or eligibility decision is
established here.

## Direct source findings and workflow consequences

Every entry was checked on **2026-10-26**. Municipal examples are explicitly local;
they do not establish another municipality's procedure.

Recheck classification, permits, residence/work entitlement where relevant, local documents, fees, timing and the current portal; forwarding does not prove tax registration or permission was granted. |
| [UStG § 14](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__14.html). Recheck invoice type, special rules, recipient/location, exemption or reverse-charge questions and applicable implementing provisions. A template or PDF does not establish that all requirements are met. |

Recheck performance date, domestic status, recipient, VAT treatment, current thresholds/transitions, format and transmission agreement. Recheck whether the event is an amendment, a new activity, a permit matter or a move to another municipality. Do not apply the within-Berlin route nationally. |

Current Berlin cessation, relocation and legal-form guidance. | Complete cessation differs from partial activity cessation. This source alone does not establish treatment for a freelancer, relief eligibility or a valid final tax computation. |
| [UStG § 18](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/__18.html). Recheck category, record date, transitional rules, open assessments/proceedings and other applicable obligations. Backup verification does not establish accounting correctness or permission to destroy originals. |

## Workflow assumptions and unverified points

The consequences above are cautious workflow applications of the cited findings,
not individual legal determinations. Confirm the entrepreneur's facts, event date,
activity and selected systems before applying them. Record the applicable period
and practical consequence with each case-specific source check.

Keep cessation notification, final reporting, settled liabilities and retained
records as separate outcomes. As a workflow safeguard, keep unpaid invoices,
refunds, contracts, assets, correspondence and unanswered authority matters
visible until resolved; the map does not establish when every obligation ends.
Check other activities of the same entrepreneur before closing shared accounts or
services. Preserve readable exports and access to original records before a
service ends, using the [recovery contract](recovery.md).

Contract interpretation, disputed claims, litigation, limitation periods,
employment classification, specialist permissions and complete closure tax
calculations were not researched to case-level completeness. Help with evidence,
questions, neutral drafts and relevant primary-source research; apply the
[legal boundaries](legal-boundaries.md) when the actual task needs legal analysis
or representation. Professional consultation remains a case-specific option;
its availability is not a substitute for useful factual preparation.

No portal login, application, employee registration, filing, payment, live
connection or authority response was tested. Official pages and laws can change;
recheck before an external action. This map introduces no fixed deadline,
retention schedule, tax formula, fee schedule or automatic legal classification.
