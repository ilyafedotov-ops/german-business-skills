# Changes, pause and closure

Use the dated [lifecycle source map](lifecycle-sources.md), reopening the competent
authority's current sources for the event/date. This is a preparation and execution
workflow using available tools, not an automatic registration, payroll, migration
or closure service. Preserve the same entrepreneur's history across all activities.

## Establish the change

Distinguish intended, actual and authority-confirmed dates. Identify affected
activities, location, legal form, existing decisions, systems and pending matters.
A pause, partial cessation, relocation and full cessation are different facts;
do not select the legal consequence from the user's informal word “closed.”

Prepare one before/after record and dependency plan. For every action record the
recipient/system, current source, needed facts, expected artifact, authorization
boundary, completion evidence and follow-up. Use existing tasks/questions and
readable documents; do not create a second lifecycle state register.

## Activity, address or legal-form changes

Check local registration and tax routes, permissions, accounts/contracts and
invoice identity affected by the specific change. Recheck how municipalities
coordinate a relocation; do not hardcode separate deregistration and registration
as a universal national sequence. Preserve previous facts/effective dates.

For a new legal entity or different legal form, identify identity, ownership,
accounting, contract and asset-transfer questions before moving records. Prepare
a transition plan and appropriate specialist/system route; do not reinterpret an
existing natural-person workspace as corporate books or claim a migration occurred.

## First employee

Establish the proposed employment relationship, start, location, existing employer
identifiers and chosen payroll support. Use current official employer resources
to identify dependent registration and reporting steps and the required evidence.
Employment classification, accident/social insurance, payroll tax and contract
questions need their own verified treatment. An application for one identifier
is not completed hiring or payroll. Prepare materials and next actions without
collecting unnecessary employee health or identity records into model context.

## Accounting or service transition

Inventory the source of truth, original documents, versions, unsettled items,
access requirements and supported exports. Follow [recovery](recovery.md) before
an authorized protected migration. Plan a cutover and read-back/reconciliation;
retain the old history and prevent duplicate entries. Current accounting writers
block changing configured authority after records exist: report that limit and
prepare the migration, never edit canonical JSON to bypass it.

## Pause or closure

Build a case-specific dependency list covering:

- Which activity stops, which continues, and the relevant authority notifications.
- Open invoices, payments/refunds, contracts, subscriptions and asset questions.
- Remaining reporting periods, corrections and authority follow-up through
  [annual workflow](annual-workflow.md); deregistration is not final tax settlement.
- Required exports, retained originals, readable access, unresolved proceedings
  and backup/restore. Do not infer permission to delete from an old document date.
- Service cancellations only after considering continuing activities, needed
  access and contractual conditions. A closure plan does not authorize cancellation.

Prepare drafts using [forms](forms.md) and [document authoring](document-authoring.md).
Act through the available verified route only within matching authorization.
Preserve each actual acknowledgment and its scope; leave unresolved matters open.
The completion report must distinguish submitted notices, observed authority
outcomes, settled operational items and continuing obligations. Do not mark the
whole entrepreneur finished while another activity or unresolved task remains.
