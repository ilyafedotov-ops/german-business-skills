# First employee: prepare a working route

Use with [changes](changes.md) and the EN/RU change-plan worksheet linked there.
This covers preparation and supported host actions, not a payroll calculator or
a certification of employment-law compliance. Self-managed work with suitable
software and an explicitly chosen service are both possible routes.

## Establish the case without collecting a personnel archive

Reuse business location/activity, intended work, actual start date, employment
category under consideration, work sector and existing employer registrations.
Identify who will calculate payroll, transmit reports and make payments. These
can be different people/systems. Confirm needed employee facts through the
appropriate protected employer flow; do not request full identity/health documents,
tax IDs or access secrets in conversation just to create a plan.

The following official sources were checked on **2026-10-26**. They describe
current processes/consolidated provisions; recheck applicability for the actual
employment start and reporting period. The practical action order is a workflow
inference, not a universal statutory checklist or fixed deadline schedule.

| Preparation item | Official source and direct finding | Concrete output and completion evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing accident-insurance registration | [DGUV: company registration](https://www.dguv.de/de/versicherung/unternehmensnummer/anmeldung/index.jsp): business registration concerns opening, not only hiring; Unternehmensnummer accompanies the jurisdiction decision. A Gewerbeanmeldung can fulfil the described notification duty. | Locate the existing institution decision and number before preparing another application. Missing records require clarification with the competent institution; do not assume the proprietor's own insurance coverage. |
| Employment establishment identifier | [BA: Betriebsnummer](https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/betriebsnummern-service/alles-wichtige): electronic application and Unternehmensnummer prerequisite since 2024; assignment confirmation is posted. | Check existing numbers and actual establishment. Prepare the official application if needed. Keep application and number-assignment evidence separate from employee registration. |
| Social-insurance reporting route | [Bundesportal/BMAS](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/DE/leistung/99107065104000): electronic reports use system-tested payroll software or a completion aid; usual collection agency is the employee's statutory health insurer, and Minijob-Zentrale for marginal employment. Some sectors need Sofortmeldung. | Establish category, sector, collection agency and chosen tool. Check the actual start-specific requirements and dates. Preserve preparation, transmission, acceptance/warnings and recurring follow-up separately. A completion aid does not establish payroll calculation. |
| Wage-tax characteristics where applicable | [ELSTER: ELStAM Arbeitgeber](https://www.elster.de/elsterweb/infoseite/elstam_%28arbeitgeber%29?locale=de_DE): retrieval uses employer access and supporting software or Mein ELSTER employer functions; relevant employee facts include date of birth, tax ID and main/secondary employment. | Verify whether this route applies to the chosen treatment. Prepare a protected data-collection checklist and account-access task. Distinguish registration, retrieval and actual use in payroll; do not expose identifiers in the plan. |
| Wage-tax reports and payment | [EStG § 41a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/estg/__41a.html): declaration and payment to the Betriebsstättenfinanzamt are distinct duties; reporting periods and the prescribed electronic dataset have specific rules. | Record the competent office, sourced period/deadline determination and chosen calculation/transmission arrangement. Preserve the observed tool output, report receipt and payment evidence separately. No formula or automatic reporting-period choice is supplied here. |

## Deliver the hiring preparation package

Use the existing change plan, with one row per applicable workstream:

- Employment classification and agreement: confirmed terms, unresolved questions,
  applicable official research, draft or chosen specialist route for the actual issue.
- Employer registrations/access: existing evidence, missing identifiers and the
  dependencies above. A number application alone cannot complete this workstream.
- Payroll calculation: selected tool/service, supported employment case and period,
  inputs still needed, actual observed result or not run. Do not calculate wages,
  contributions or tax with newly invented formulas.
- Reporting and payment: exact route/recipient, responsible person, preview,
  authorization and evidence expected. Check sector-specific timing before work starts.
- Recurring work: sourced dates, responsible owner and retained records; use existing
  tasks. A saved due date is not a delivered reminder or functioning payroll process.

When facts are incomplete, finish the plan and unaffected drafts, ask the decisive
question and retain unresolved rows. For a synthetic first hire in an established
business, first checking the old DGUV correspondence can avoid a duplicate opening
application. This example does not determine any real company's registration state.

No login, application, employee registration, payroll run, payment or reporting
was tested by this source research. Employment status, individual deadlines,
contracts, insurance treatment and nonstandard cases need case-specific checks.
