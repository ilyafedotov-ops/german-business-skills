# Legal questions and service boundaries

Checked **2026-10-25** against current official sources. Use for non-tax legal questions, threatened disputes, permissions or requests to operate a service for other people. Recheck the provision and its effective period for the user's actual facts; a current source does not establish the rule for an earlier event. For tax-specific professional rules, use [German rules](german-rules.md#free-software-and-individual-tax-services).

## Establish whose affairs and which task

Distinguish a person using self-help software for their own business from an operator offering individual advice, representation or managed administration to clients. Publishing a static skill does not by itself establish that its author provides every downstream service. Conversely, a free licence, workflow name or disclaimer does not establish permission to provide a regulated service.

RDG §2 concerns individual legal examination in concrete third-party matters; §3 requires an applicable permission. The ancillary-service and unpaid-service provisions in §§5–6 have conditions. Assess the actual service, responsible people, qualifications and supervision rather than inferring an exemption from “free” or “part of bookkeeping”. The separate StBerG rules still apply to tax assistance. Sources: [RDG §2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/rdg/__2.html), [§3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/rdg/__3.html), [§5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/rdg/__5.html), [§6](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/rdg/__6.html).

Continue useful explanations, factual preparation and arithmetic for ordinary self-help. If a user plans to advise clients, prepare the proposed service scope and the precise authorization/supervision questions for qualified review before claiming that operating model is permitted. This review issue does not prevent a synthetic demonstration or organizing the operator's own records.

## Triage the affected issue

| Trigger | Useful preparation | Specific review issue |
|---|---|---|
| Contract clause, cancellation, liability or threatened claim | Identify contract/version, parties, relevant dates, requested outcome and disputed wording; explain sourced general rules | Enforceability, legal strategy or an individual rights determination outside this sole-trader administration workflow |
| Employee dispute, residence permission, regulated trade or insolvency | Collect only necessary facts and official correspondence; identify the competent authority or suitable specialist | Applicable permissions, employment rights or specialist procedure; a Gewerbeanmeldung alone does not resolve these |
| Debt collection or representation for someone else | Identify whose claim, intended recipient, role and proposed action | Authority to provide the service and to represent that person; RDG separately addresses independent collection services |
| Authority letter or approaching deadline | Preserve the document; establish issue type, stated date, receipt/notification evidence and prior actions; prepare a dated action list | Applicable time-computation rule, remedy and timely response; tax issues route to the tax workflow |

Name the narrow unresolved conclusion and the evidence needed. Continue unaffected extraction, a factual chronology, questions and clearly marked drafts. A request to understand a clause is not automatically a request for representation. For a consequential legal assessment outside this release's scope, prepare a focused handoff instead of presenting a definitive answer.

For example, if a letter appears to require action tomorrow, surface that urgency immediately, verify the source and date basis, and identify the responsible next contact. Prepare the necessary facts and a reviewable response promptly. Do not wait for the rest of the bookkeeping month, invent a deadline from an old template, or claim that drafting preserves a remedy. Sending, accepting terms or making declarations follows [forms](forms.md) and the actual authorization scope.

## Use AI with an understood scope

For a user's first consequential workflow, explain briefly in their chosen language: the assistant can misread evidence or cite an inapplicable rule; local files may be sent to a remote model; and “draft”, “reviewed”, “submitted” and “authority response received” describe different evidence. Use a synthetic example to show the review step and ask for the missing task facts rather than requesting blanket acceptance of every risk.

For organizations, identify who uses the AI on their behalf and the practical guidance they need for those tasks. AI Act duties depend on the actual provider/deployer role and intended use; static skill distribution alone does not settle either. A startup/bookkeeping workflow is not automatically a high-risk use case. A future hosted service or employment/credit decision feature needs its own assessment. Keep an appropriate record of guidance; do not label this short introduction a compliance certificate. Source: [European Commission AI-literacy FAQ, Article 4](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/faqs/ai-literacy-questions-answers) and [current AI Act, especially Articles 3–6](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A02024R1689-20260727).
