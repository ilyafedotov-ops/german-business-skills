# Service-selection evidence and boundaries

Checked **2026-10-25** for German banking/insurance selection. Legal links refer to the consolidated text checked that day, not a promise about later changes or historical years. Guidance publication dates and policy editions are stated where available. These sources support preparation and verification; they do not establish a particular user's eligibility, legal duties or insurance coverage.

## Insurance distribution and professional checks

- [§ 34d GewO](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__34d.html), current consolidated text: commercial insurance intermediation and insurance advice are separately regulated; exemptions have conditions. The adviser under paragraph 2 is paid by the client and must not receive insurer benefits. Paragraph 1 also includes criteria-based product information/rankings where users can conclude contracts directly or indirectly through the medium. **Consequence:** calling an activity “software” or “self-help” does not itself establish an exemption. Treat commercial personalised advice, affiliate/referral arrangements and contract assistance as requiring activity-specific regulatory review before offering them. A disclaimer cannot determine the classification.
- [IHK Berlin: permission scope](https://www.ihk.de/berlin/service-und-beratung/recht-und-steuern/gewerberecht/erlaubnis-registrierungsverfahren-ihk/erlaubnispflicht-6178346), current guidance checked above: a *Tippgeber* only identifies opportunities or introduces contacts without specification of a particular product. **Example:** forwarding a product-specific recommendation with a purchase route cannot simply be labelled an unregulated introduction. Data-sharing permission is a separate question.
- [VVG §§ 59–62](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/vvg_2008/BJNR263110007.html), current consolidated text: distinguish representatives, brokers and advisers; § 60 addresses the market/information basis, § 61 needs, advice reasons and documentation, § 62 timing/form. Statutory exceptions and waiver rules exist. **Consequence:** request the professional's role, remuneration, market restrictions, reasons and documentation; do not encourage a waiver as a shortcut. These professional duties are not proof that this package is licensed.
- [DIHK public intermediary register](https://www.vermittlerregister.info/) and [DIHK register guidance](https://www.dihk.de/de/serviceportal/fuer-gewerbetreibende-unternehmer/gewerbliche-vermittlung-von-finanz-und-versicherungsprodukten-141736), current live registers/guidance: match the actual person/entity, registration number and role. [BaFin company database explanation](https://bafin.de/EN/die-bafin/publikationen-daten/datenbanken-uebersichten/unternehmenssuche/unternehmenssuche_node_en.html) distinguishes German authorisation from EEA cross-border/branch notification. **Consequence:** check the risk-carrying insurer separately from the intermediary; a brand-name miss or absence from a domestic-only subset proves neither illegality nor authorisation. Resolve entity, home-state authority and German operating basis; record unresolved checks as unknown. A register entry does not certify policy suitability.

## Identify the obligation and loss before the product

- [Federal portal: liability and compulsory cover](https://verwaltung.bund.de/leistungsverzeichnis/de/rechte-und-pflichten/102835435), published **2022-12-20**, checked above: professional insurance duties differ by occupation, Land and sometimes legal form. Verify the current governing law/chamber rule rather than treating this directory as the final rule. **Example:** a client-required liability certificate is a contractual requirement; it is not automatically a statutory duty for every freelancer.
- [GDV professional-liability explanation](https://www.dieversicherer.de/versicherer/beruf/berufshaftpflichtversicherung), **2024-03-15**: business/office liability addresses bodily/property injury; professional liability can include pure financial loss depending on the profession/policy. Names overlap. **Example:** damaging a client's laptop and causing a loss through faulty advice require distinct coverage checks; private liability is not a substitute for business cover.
- [GDV condition directory](https://www.gdv.de/gdv/service/musterbedingungen), current directory: cyber, property/equipment, interruption and legal-expenses conditions are separate categories. **Workflow inference:** compare third-party liability, own assets, restoration/incident response, interruption and legal costs separately; ask which losses the business can retain. No universal bundle follows from being self-employed.
- [DGUV: entrepreneurs](https://www.dguv.de/de/versicherung/versicherte_personen/vers-unternehmer/index.jsp), current guidance: statutory accident cover may arise by law or an accident insurer's statutes; many entrepreneurs can insure voluntarily. [DRV: self-employed](https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Rente/Arbeitnehmer-und-Selbststaendige/03_Selbststaendige/selbststaendige_node) identifies occupation/status-dependent pension obligations. [BMG: insured groups](https://www.bundesgesundheitsministerium.de/gesetzlich-versicherte) explains health-insurance membership routes. **Consequence:** route health/care, pension and statutory accident status separately to the responsible bodies; neither “freelancer” nor secondary employment resolves every obligation. This is administrative triage, not medical advice; do not collect diagnoses for initial selection.

## Compare contract evidence

[GDV AVB Cyber, February 2024](https://www.gdv.de/resource/blob/6100/a0fed56c4947751cdc20b5206c171d98/01-allgemeine-versicherungsbedingungen-fuer-die-cyberrisiko-versicherung-avb-cyber--data.pdf) and [AVB Benachteiligungen, November 2025](https://www.gdv.de/resource/blob/5914/360f5e3cd369eaafca68991c58a564a0/03-allgemeine-bedingungen-zur-haftpflichtversicherung-von-anspruechen-aus-benachteiligungen-avb-benachteiligungen-data.pdf) are voluntary models, not every insurer's contract. The latter illustrates claims-made, retroactive cover and reporting after expiry. **Consequence:** obtain the actual dated wording, schedule and endorsements. Compare insured activities/people, exclusions, territory/jurisdiction, limits/sublimits/annual aggregates, deductibles, duties, waiting periods and the applicable event trigger. Check occurrence, breach or claims-made timing and retroactive/tail arrangements where relevant; do not assume identical triggers across products. Marketing or model wording does not establish purchased cover. Record missing clauses as unknown, not covered.

## Account protection

[BaFin deposit protection](https://bafin.de/DE/verbraucherinnen-verbraucher/themen-finanzprodukte/geldanlage/einlagensicherung-anlegerentschaedigung/einlagensicherung-anlegerentschaedigung.html) describes the usual statutory ceiling of **EUR 100,000 per depositor per bank**, subject to eligibility/exclusions and special cases. [BaFin ZAG guidance](https://bafin.de/SharedDocs/Veroeffentlichungen/DE/Merkblatt/mb_111222_zag.html) distinguishes payment/e-money safeguarding under §§ 13/13a ZAG from deposit protection. **Consequence:** verify the contracting entity, institution type and actual funds arrangement; an IBAN or fintech brand does not prove deposit protection. Do not equate safeguarding with a universal EUR 100,000 guarantee.
