# Insurance needs, evidence and comparison

Use for the user's own business-insurance questions, existing-policy gaps or comparison of offers. Read [the shared provider workflow](providers.md) and the dated [official-source notes](service-research-sources.md). Help the user understand needs and compare actual terms; do not assume everyone needs every policy or that “freelancer” determines insurance obligations.

## Establish the exposed risk and existing cover

Reuse the actual activity, countries and contracts. Ask the small number of missing facts that could change the answer: services/products delivered, premises/equipment, staff/subcontractors, client types and locations, possible physical versus financial loss, contractual insurance requirements, required start date and existing policy scope. Distinguish statutory/occupation-specific obligations, client-contract requirements and optional risk choices. Verify any claimed obligation with its actual legal/professional basis and scope; generic mandatory-insurance lists are insufficient.

Review existing policy wording/schedule/endorsements before suggesting replacement. Identify insured person/business/activity, period, limits, territory and declared facts; do not treat a general household/private-liability policy as established business cover. Use a summary or redacted evidence where sufficient. Health, disability and other underwriting can involve sensitive information: do not ask for diagnoses, full medical histories or identity documents merely to make an initial shortlist. Accurate sensitive disclosures belong in an appropriate official/professional application route when needed; never invent or omit facts to obtain a cheaper premium.

## Separate the policy purposes

| Area to investigate when relevant | Distinction to preserve |
| --- | --- |
| Business/public liability (Betriebshaftpflicht) | Injury/property damage and associated loss; does not automatically establish cover for pure financial loss from professional advice/software |
| Professional/financial-loss liability (Berufshaftpflicht / Vermögensschadenhaftpflicht) | Covered professional activities and financial-loss events depend on exact wording; product names alone do not establish coverage or a legal obligation |
| Cyber | Response services, own loss, third-party liability and security prerequisites vary; not a substitute for security or backup, and not automatically part of professional liability |
| Equipment/property/business interruption | Insured assets/perils, valuation, waiting periods and interruption trigger/indemnity period; interruption is not always triggered by any revenue decline |
| Legal expenses | Covered disputes/activities, exclusions, waiting periods and existing-event rules; separate from liability damages and the defence provisions of another policy |
| Health, pension, accident and income protection | Separate personal/social-insurance questions; actual employment/activity/status and competent authority matter. Do not treat these as interchangeable optional business add-ons or make medical recommendations |

These are investigation categories, not coverage promises. Some policies combine elements; compare the actual contract and avoid overlapping purchases. Use existing-data onboarding to retrieve authorized policy documents when necessary, without sending them to insurers by default.

## Compare the actual offer

For each candidate record insurer and any intermediary role, exact product/tariff/version, dated policy wording, schedule/endorsements, quote conditions, underwriting status, quote validity and coverage start. A public “from” price is not an individualized quote, and a quote is not accepted cover. Check the appropriate official supervisory/intermediary information with its scope; register presence does not certify suitability, and a single unsuccessful register search does not prove an EEA provider unauthorized.

Treat these as hard checks where material:

- Insured activity and persons, work performed through subcontractors, excluded services, contractual liability and required client limits.
- Territory **and** jurisdiction/claims location, including explicit US/Canada exclusions where relevant; a globally accessible website does not establish global insurance.
- Per-claim and annual aggregate limits, sublimits, deductibles, whether defence costs reduce limits, and relevant accumulation rules.
- Event/claim trigger and timing, retroactive dates, prior/known circumstances and extended reporting/run-off where relevant. Do not assume all policies use the same trigger or invent continuity when switching.
- Security, notification and cooperation conditions; cancellation, automatic renewal, payment frequency, waiting periods and material-change duties.
- Total payable premium, tax/fees, instalment surcharges and normal renewal basis. Keep deductibles and uninsured loss as separate exposure, not a guaranteed annual premium cost. Do not assume VAT recovery for insurance tax or financial/insurance products.

An unresolved or failed material coverage condition blocks an unconditional fit recommendation even when the premium is lowest. Explain the precise wording/endorsement or insurer clarification needed; continue comparing unaffected facts. Do not declare a claim covered or promise an insurer's decision from a brochure.

## Useful recommendation and next step

Summarize risk priorities and what existing cover already does. Recommend the best-supported option or a conditional route, explain what would change the recommendation, and include retaining existing cover/buying nothing when supported. If no offer meets the stated contract/territory/activity requirement, say so and prepare a specific clarification request rather than selecting the cheapest unsuitable one.

Record the user's actual decision separately. Research does not authorize transmitting health/business details for a quote, becoming the user's broker, accepting terms, cancelling an old policy or binding a new one. Before an authorized switch, reconcile old/new dates and relevant timing/known-circumstance conditions; do not create a coverage gap. Professional help is a focused option for unresolved interpretation/regulated distribution, not a substitute for providing useful factual research. For an actual claim or dispute, route the specific legal issue through [legal boundaries](legal-boundaries.md).
