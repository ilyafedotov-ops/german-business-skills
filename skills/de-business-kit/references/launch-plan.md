# From activity idea to a reviewable launch plan

Use with [formation](formation.md) and the dated [lifecycle source map](lifecycle-sources.md).
This is agent-led preparation, not a registration or forecasting engine.

Establish the actual offer, customers/countries, delivery location, known
qualifications or restrictions, intended start and other activities. Separate
ideas from signed commitments, forecasts from actual transactions, and the user's
preference from an authority's classification. Do useful work with incomplete facts.

Prepare one readable plan in the user's language with:

- The proposed activity and alternatives that materially change the next step.
- Established facts, assumptions and decisive unanswered questions with sources.
- Necessary investigations: classification, permissions, local registration/tax
  route, insurance/service needs and data handling for this case.
- Dependencies: which fact or outcome is needed before each action, who acts,
  what document is produced and what evidence will establish completion.
- Launch resources: existing tools/contracts, essential initial/recurring costs,
  revenue assumptions supplied by the user and uncertain amounts. Do not invent
  customer demand or estimate tax from revenue alone. Use [estimates](tax-estimates.md)
  for sourced tax inputs/tools, and suitable available tools for financial scenarios.
- The first useful action and a later follow-up list; not every potential service
  is needed before the business can ask its first question or prepare a form.

For each form use current official help and confirmed facts. Keep field proposals,
review, authorization, submission and observed authority outcome separate. A missing
classification must not block unaffected factual fields. Registration confirmation,
a tax identifier, an enabled portal account and an operational banking/accounting
service are distinct results.

Without a workspace, deliver the plan in conversation or an authorized readable
file. With one, record tasks/questions/decisions through [workspace](workspace.md)
and retain the plan as evidence. Use existing effective-dated facts and activity
IDs; do not create a separate entrepreneur for a brand or additional activity.
